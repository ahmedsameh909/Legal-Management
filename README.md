# Legal Management Module for Odoo

A custom Odoo module designed to streamline legal operations, contract lifecycle management, case tracking, and document workflows for corporate legal departments and law firms.

---

## 🚀 Key Features

* **Case & Litigation Management:** Track legal cases, court hearings, hearing outcomes, and case statuses seamlessly.
* **Contract Management:** Manage contract lifecycles, expiration alerts, renewals, and partner obligations.
* **Document Management:** Securely store, categorize, and track versions of legal documents and court filings.
* **Task & Deadline Tracking:** Assign tasks to legal advisors, set deadlines, and receive automated reminders for critical dates.
* **Access Control & Security:** Granular access rights for Legal Managers, Advisors, and Internal Users to ensure data confidentiality.

---

## 🛠️ Requirements

* **Odoo Version:** 18.0 (or 17.0 depending on your environment)
* **Python Dependencies:** None (Standard Odoo Framework)
